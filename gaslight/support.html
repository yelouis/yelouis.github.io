Gaslight Parlor — Help & Support

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Gaslight Parlor is a party game of truth and forgery for three or more players, each on their own phone. Questions, problems or feedback? Email us and we'll get back to you.

Contact: chengluye@gmail.com

How to play

  1. One player taps CREATE ROOM and shares the four-letter room code.
  2. Everyone else enters the code and taps JOIN ROOM, then I'M READY.
  3. The host chooses a deck and the house rules, then taps START GAME.
  4. Forge: write convincing lies for your friends' prompts.
  5. Confess: write the truth for your own.
  6. Vote: for each card, find the real answer among the forgeries.
  7. Reveal: see who found the truth and who was fooled. If you were fooled, you can accuse the forger before the unmask window closes.

The full rules are in the game: tap READ MANUAL on the title screen.

Common questions

How many people can play?

At least three. Each player needs their own phone, or a web browser.

Do I need an account?

No. The game signs you in anonymously in the background.

Can friends without the app join?

Yes. They open gaslight-46368.web.app in a web browser and enter your room code.

I closed the app in the middle of a game. Can I get back in?

Yes. Open the game again on the same device and it returns you to your room.

Is there content for adults?

One deck, "Rated R NSFW", is for adults, and the host chooses whether to use it. The other four decks are rated PG.

Someone wrote something offensive. What can I do?

  • Report it: during the reveal, tap Report on the answer, or Report this prompt under a prompt a player wrote. Reports come to us for review.
  • Remove the player: in the parlour, the host can remove a player. A removed player can't rejoin that room.
  • Email us with the room code and what happened.

Names, answers and custom prompts are also checked against a list of slurs before they are accepted.

How do I delete my data?

Rooms are deleted automatically, normally within two days, and anonymous sign-ins after 24 hours without use. To have a room deleted sooner, email us the room code. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

The sound is too much.

Tap the bell icon in the lobby or on the reveal screen to mute or unmute.
