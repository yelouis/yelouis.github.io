Gaslight Parlor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Gaslight Parlor is a party game you play with friends. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, why, and for how long. It applies to the iOS app and to the web version.

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never give us an email address, a password, or a phone number.
  • We store the display name you type, the answers you write during a game, and your score — for as long as the game lasts.
  • Game rooms and everything in them are deleted automatically, normally within two days of your game.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

What the app collects

WhatWhy
An anonymous player ID — a random identifier created by Firebase Anonymous Authentication. So the game can tell players apart, keep your seat if the app restarts, and stop anyone else acting as you. It is not connected to an Apple ID, email address, or any other account.
The display name you type, plus the colour and token you pick. So other players in your room can see who wrote what.
What you write during a game — your truthful answers, your invented ones, any prompts you contribute to a custom deck, and your votes and accusations. This is the game itself. Other players in your room see these as the round unfolds.
Gameplay counters — your score, how many players you fooled, and how many times you were fooled. Scoring, standings, and the end-of-game honours.
A "last seen" timestamp, updated periodically while the app is open. So the game can tell whether you are still connected and can end or continue a match sensibly if someone leaves.

What the app does not collect

  • No email address, password, phone number, or real name — sign-in is anonymous.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location. The iOS app asks for one permission, and only if you use it: add-only access to Photos, the first time you choose Save Image for the end-of-game Case File. It never reads your photo library.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK. We do not measure how you use the app.
  • No tracking, and no sharing of data with data brokers or advertisers.

Who processes the data

The app uses Google Firebase — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Functions — to run games. Google processes this data on our behalf as a service provider, on servers in the United States. Google's handling of it is governed by the Firebase Privacy and Security terms.

No other third party receives any of it.

Keeping the game safe

  • A word filter. Display names, answers and custom prompts are checked on our server against a list of slurs before they are used. Nothing is sent outside Firebase to do this.
  • Reports. Any player can report an answer or a custom prompt from the reveal screen. A report stores the reported text, the prompt it was written for, the room code and the time, so we can review it. It does not record who sent the report or who wrote the text, and it is deleted automatically 30 days after the latest report of that text.
  • Removed players. If the host removes a player, our server keeps that player's anonymous ID with the room so they cannot rejoin it. It is deleted with the room.

How long it is kept

DataHow long
A game room and everything in it — names, answers, prompts, votes and scores The room expires 8 hours after its last activity, and an automatic daily cleanup deletes it — normally within two days of your game. If you leave a game, your player record is removed at that point; answers you already gave stay in that game's record until the room is deleted.
The anonymous player ID Deleted automatically after 24 hours without use, at the next daily cleanup.
Reports Deleted automatically 30 days after the latest report of that text.
Server logs Google Cloud keeps standard request logs for up to 30 days. We do not use them to identify anyone.

Because there are no accounts, nothing persists between games beyond this.

What stays on your device

The app stores a random player ID, your current room code, a rejoin token and your sound setting locally, so it can put you back into your game if the app is closed and reopened. Deleting the app removes them. iOS may keep the anonymous sign-in in its Keychain after the app is deleted, but the account it points to is deleted on our side after 24 hours without use. The end-of-game "Case File" image is generated on your device — on iOS it is handed to the system share sheet, and on the web it is saved to your downloads. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Children

Gaslight Parlor is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. The game's prompt decks include an adults-only option, which the host chooses when setting up a room. If you believe a child has played, contact us with the room code and we will delete that room's data.

Your choices

Because we hold no account, there is no profile to log into or delete. If you want the data from a game removed sooner than the automatic deletion, leave the room — or contact us with the 4-letter room code and we will delete it. For help with the game, see the support page.
